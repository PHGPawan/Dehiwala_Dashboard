Urban Heat Island Effect

Key Potentials & Opportunities
K1
Strong Walkable Local Accessibility
High ValueMobility
Evidence
500m closeness centrality ranks Dehiwala Urban Area as the highest accessibility node in the study area
Dense mixed-use base within walkable radius means most daily needs can theoretically be met on foot
High Shannon Entropy near the station shows existing variety of land uses supporting active frontages
How to Leverage
→
Reclaim pavement from parked vehicles and widen footpaths along the top 3 closeness streets to create a walkable commercial spine
→
Market Dehiwala as a 15-minute neighbourhood — low car-dependency model within existing urban structure
K2
Transit-Ready Mixed Land Use Base
High ValueLand Use
Evidence
Shannon Entropy Index of 0.72 near the station — among the highest in the study area, indicating genuine land use diversity
12 distinct land use categories present within a 500m radius of Dehiwala station
Commercial, residential, institutional and cultural uses all within walkable distance of the rail stop
How to Leverage
→
Develop a Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) masterplan centred on Dehiwala station — protect land use mix while enabling vertical densification
→
Designate a 400m TOD zone with mixed-use zoning overlays protecting ground-floor retail and active uses
K3
Regional Connectivity Asset
StrategicConnectivity
Evidence
5000m betweenness centrality identifies Dehiwala as a key node on the Colombo–South Coast corridor
Rail connection provides direct access to Colombo Fort, Mount Lavinia and Kalutara
2000m betweenness shows secondary connections to Nugegoda and Kohuwala commercial nodes
How to Leverage
→
Position Dehiwala as a sub-regional service centre — attract higher-order commercial, health and education facilities enabled by regional accessibility
→
Coordinate with Western Province transport planning to designate Dehiwala as a bus-rail interchange node
K4
Under-Utilised Rail Catchment for TOD
StrategicTransit
Evidence
Dehiwala station is on the mainline Colombo–Galle railway — high-frequency service, central location
Current station forecourt is dominated by informal parking — not utilised as a transit-oriented public space
800m walking catchment covers majority of the study area — effectively the entire population is within rail access
How to Leverage
→
Station forecourt redesign — convert informal parking to an active public plaza with shade, seating and last-mile connectivity (cycling, tuk-tuks)
→
Enable mixed-use intensification within 400m of station entrance — residential above ground-floor commercial to increase ridership and housing supply
K5
Southeastern Wetland as Cooling Anchor
EnvironmentalGreen Infra
Evidence
OSR of 0.65 in the southeastern wetland zone — the only major open permeable surface in the study area
Surface temperatures in coastal/wetland zone approx 32°C vs 47°C at Galle Road — 15°C differential
Sea breeze corridor from the southwest identified in wind simulation — wetland acts as cooling relay point
How to Leverage
→
Protect and extend wetland edge — designate buffer zone with no-build setback and native planting to enhance evapotranspiration and air cooling
→
Design a green breeze corridor connecting wetland to the station via a tree-lined public path — channelling cool air into the urban core
